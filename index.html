Survey Methodology & QA/QC

Standardized 6-Step Execution Lifecycle

Rigorous quality control protocols ensuring precision from initial project reconnaissance to final technical review and drawing validation.

01
PROJECT STUDY & RECONNAISSANCE

Comprehensive review of survey scope, existing maps, alignment corridors, site terrain access, and geodetic reference marks.

02
CONTROL ESTABLISHMENT

Primary and secondary geodetic network setup with dual-frequency DGPS / GNSS base-rover observations and TBM level shifting.

03
DETAILED FIELD OBSERVATIONS

High-precision Total Station traverse observations, digital auto levelling, cross-sectional measurements, and RTK aerial drone flights.

04
DATA PROCESSING & QA/QC

Point cloud filtering, raw data reduction, level loop adjustments, closure error checks, and coordinate verification.

05
CAD / GIS DRAFTING & MODELING

Standardized AutoCAD drawings, 3D DTM surface models, corridor profiles, L & cross sections, and GIS geospatial databases.

06
FINAL REVIEW & REPORT SUBMISSION

Independent quality audit by proprietor & technical lead, drawing sign-off, client reviews, and engineering-ready deliverable handover.
